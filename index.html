Straight talk

Diagnose first. Then prescribe.

Burnout mostly comes from how an organization runs: workload, priorities, and management. Sometimes overwork is causing the friction, not the other way around.

If that is the case, sending exhausted engineers to training tells them they are the problem. So for larger departments we start with a paid diagnostic and say plainly what training can and cannot fix before you commit to anything bigger.
